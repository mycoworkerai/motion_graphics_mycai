# Quality control

Review the rendered video at full size and at its intended playback speed. Source code inspection alone cannot establish visual quality.

## Creative review

- Does the opening earn attention quickly?
- Is the central message accurate and immediately understandable?
- Does each scene have one clear narrative purpose?
- Can every line be read comfortably?
- Is the visual hierarchy obvious without explanation?
- Does each animation have a meaningful purpose?
- Is the pacing varied, with enough stillness?
- Are transitions coherent and free of accidental jumps?
- Are colors, typography, and spacing consistent with the project design system?
- Are margins and contrast appropriate for the target platform?
- Does the ending provide a satisfying conclusion or clear CTA?

## Technical review

- Do composition dimensions match the brief?
- Are fps and duration correct?
- Are frame ranges and animation boundaries valid?
- Are animations deterministic and reproducible?
- Are assets available locally and referenced correctly?
- Does the final render match the requested dimensions, fps, and duration?
- Did the repository's available lint/build checks pass?

## Final rule

Remove effects or elements that do not improve communication. Verify facts and product claims against the brief or source material. Report unrun checks as unrun; never imply that a preview or render was inspected if it was not.
