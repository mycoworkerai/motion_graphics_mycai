# Quality control

Review the rendered video at full size and at its intended playback speed.

Checklist:
- Does the opening earn attention quickly?
- Is the central message accurate and immediately understandable?
- Can every line be read comfortably?
- Is the hierarchy obvious without explanation?
- Does each animation have a purpose?
- Is the pacing varied, with enough stillness?
- Are transitions coherent and free of accidental jumps?
- Are colors and type consistent with the project design system?
- Are margins and contrast appropriate for the target platform?
- Does the ending provide a satisfying conclusion or clear CTA?
- Does the final render match the requested dimensions, fps, and duration?

Remove effects or elements that do not improve communication. Verify facts and product claims against the brief or source material.
