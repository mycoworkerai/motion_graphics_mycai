# Motion principles

Motion should communicate. Each movement should reveal, emphasize, connect, transform, or transition.

Use anticipation sparingly, clear entry and exit states, consistent easing, and a deliberate rhythm of activity and stillness. Avoid animating every element simultaneously.

Maintain continuity between scenes through shared direction, scale, timing, shape, or color. Prefer controlled, readable motion over constant bounce or overshoot.

In implementation, use Remotion's frame-driven animation model; do not rely on CSS transitions, timers, or nondeterministic browser behavior.
