# Kinetic typography

Typography is the primary visual material, not a caption layered over animation.

Establish a type hierarchy through size, weight, width, spacing, alignment, and position. Animate text in meaningful units — word, phrase, or line — based on reading order and emphasis.

Protect legibility: avoid excessive movement, overly tight timing, low contrast, and competing text animations. Keep key copy on screen long enough to read at the intended viewing size.

Use the project's existing font tokens and do not introduce extra typefaces without a reason.
