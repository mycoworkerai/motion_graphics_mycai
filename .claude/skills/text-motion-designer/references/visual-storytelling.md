# Visual storytelling

A short video needs a readable narrative arc: hook, context, development, payoff, and (when appropriate) call to action.

Give each scene one job. Use visual progression to make the idea easier to understand: contrast, sequence, transformation, scale, grouping, or cause and effect.

Keep on-screen copy short. Split complex statements into beats instead of shrinking type. Let important ideas breathe; a pause can carry more weight than another transition.
